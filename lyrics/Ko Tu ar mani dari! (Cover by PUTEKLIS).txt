Read my thoughts, read them out loud.
My world is held by the same whales that carry yours around.
With you in mind, I lose my ground.
There’s something I don’t say to you,
each time your eyes catch mine anew.
You can—
Oh God, what is it you do to me?

For just a moment — short, but truly real,
I taste something sweet in the laughter you conceal.
And that sparkle in your eyes —
I wouldn’t trade it for the skies.

I wake each morning hardly having slept,
so magical each second that I’ve kept,
when your eyes find mine again and stay.
Still, there’s something I can’t say,
each time your gaze meets mine that way.
You can—
Oh God, what is it you do to me?

For just a moment — short, but truly real,
I taste something sweet in the laughter you conceal.
And that sparkle in your eyes —
I wouldn’t trade it for the skies.
