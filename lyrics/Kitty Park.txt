For children – swings, a merry ground,
for little ones – a slide to bound,
for birds and beasts – a magic lark,
all belong in Kitty Park.

Hedgehogs, woodpeckers, rabbits, mice,
spin on rides not once, but twice.

Past the fox and past the hound,
the bunny takes the train around.

Fox says: “Stop that endless shout,
come with me, let’s drive about!”

Two strict cats with tickets near
tell the elephant, with a sneer:

“Go through jungles, fields, or dark,
you’re too big for Kitty Park.”

While the cats keep saying “No!”,
the baby elephant weeps so.

Kitty whispers: *“That’s not right,
hurting little ones in spite.

Even if he looks so tall,
he’s just four years old, that’s all.”*

Frogs or storks, in coats of grey,
whether woods or streets you stay,
homes of meadow, town or ark —
all are welcome, Kitty Park.

Māris Čaklais - Minkuparks
