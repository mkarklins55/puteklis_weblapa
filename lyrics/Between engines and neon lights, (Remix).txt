"Cuckoo in the City"

Between engines and neon lights,
on corners frozen stiff,
between roars and whispers,
I heard your voice –
a cuckoo’s song,
soft like snow on metal.

What are you doing here,
barefoot in the city’s frost?
That soft "cuckoo"...
it’s not loud enough for this century.
We buried it
somewhere deep
in the corner of a tired heart.

When the frost eased a little,
it bloomed again –
like a stubborn narcissus
between concrete cracks.

We gave it to a girl
who pressed it between flowers –
and it dried there,
slowly,
quietly.

Now I walk alone
past signs that say “Market” and “Circus”,
everyone buying noise and chaos,
and I’m holding your "cuckoo"
like it’s a secret
no one wants to hear.

And when the girl shrugs and walks away,
I’ll stay a little longer
because no one else
understands
what your voice meant
to me.
