VERSE 1

I’m heading home this winter night,
The road feels long, the lights feel slow,
Every mile pulls me closer now,
To the faces that I know.

The engine hums, the radio plays,
I let the hours slide,
I sing this tune to ease the road,
While the city fades behind.

CHORUS

I’m on my way home for Christmas,
Through the dark and through the calm,
It might take time, but I’ll get there,
I’m moving on.

I’m on my way home for Christmas,
Every mile feels like a sign,
Driving through the quiet night,
This road is mine.

VERSE 2

Bumper to bumper, red lights glow,
The streets are standing still,
But somewhere soon the road opens wide,
And the air begins to chill.

I’ll set my feet on familiar ground,
Where the noise falls away,
Where the night feels warm again,
And the world feels safe.

BRIDGE

A thousand moments ride with me,
Reflections in the glass,
I catch a glimpse of the car next door,
He’s doing just the same.

Same tired eyes, same silent wish,
Same hope inside the flame.

FINAL CHORUS / OUTRO

I’m on my way home for Christmas,
The night is soft, the city sleeps,
I slow it down, I breathe it in,
These memories I keep.

I’m on my way home for Christmas,
Nothing rushed, nothing forced,
Just wheels on snow and fading lights,
On this long road home.
