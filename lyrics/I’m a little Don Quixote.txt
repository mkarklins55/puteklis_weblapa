I’m a little Don Quixote,
Proud to never quite confess.
The road alone won’t tell you where to go,
And somewhere, someone always aches –
And home’s not always where you rest.

I’m a little Don Juan,
Self-deception branded on my brow.
There’ll be regrets, my heart may not forgive,
So take the stars before someone else
Scoops them up and weighs them down.

We can hide there – let the wind hold us,
Cradled in the pulse of night.
In the flicker of a thousand lights,
We’ll slip the weight from our hearts
Like silk through the sand –
Let it fall through the sand.

I’m a little lost and cheated,
Roused from winter’s hush.
Like a bear that doesn’t understand
Why we must learn to live
With someone always sure they know best.

I’m a little hidden still,
My heart afraid to speak.
You say: don’t be surprised if fear
Never leaves, never sleeps –
But how to know if there’s room in the sky
For me, even in the rain?

Fear that the morning will drag us under,
Too clean to hold us close.
The night won’t know our names anymore –
The night won’t know our names anymore.
