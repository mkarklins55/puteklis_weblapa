Good thing day still turns to night —
else this world would crack:
a no-world, a howl-world —
just one
long
scream.
In a rat pack you're a rat,
and I'm a rat-man too.

I slam into corners,
walls, cages —
my brain pan
fills
with lead.
Then —
one puff,
and you steal yourself from you.

A carriage, a coachman, reins that shake,
the porch dissolves into a flight through untime.
Varieté, golden rails, delirium.
A can-can of tails flattened thin,
rising higher, rushing faster,
spiraling up
into gilded agony —
we'll meet there.

We’re already there:
in a poppy field.
