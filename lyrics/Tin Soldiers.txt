Tin Soldiers

Shimmer like a golden fish-scale glow,
Fame like a star — falling, fading slow.
Take your little tin soldiers in your hand,
Come play with me on the stairs where I stand.

Where are your generals now? Where did they go?
If they still breathe — which roads do they know?
Take your little tin soldiers, worn and bent,
Come to my steps, like in dreams we once went.

They'll bury you, they'll bury me… and kittens,
like children, will sip milk from broken dishes.
But don't be sad. Just bring your toys,
Come to my stairs. We'll make no noise.

Atdzejots Māris Čaklais "Alvas zaldātiņi"
