Blue Screen Fires

(Intro — whispered, rhythmic)
Artificial intelligence
breathes inside the monitors,
a late bus
glides through suburban fog.

Someone still forgot
to turn off the office lights.

(Verse 1)
The robot writes emails
instead of me.
I only reread them
to see if there’s still
a human left inside.

The intern never comes.
His chair
slowly gathers dust.

On the night shift
server fans
hum like old bees.

(Chorus)
And we still dance
between blue screen fires,
between data and silence,
between voices without bodies.

Somewhere far away
a human still echoes.

(Verse 2)
The translator corrects sentences
where the machine
translated sadness
too perfectly.

The accountant
no longer touches paper,
only slowly
scrolls life downward.

And in the Gauja mist
electricity crackles
like an ancient pagan radio.

(Chorus)
And we still dance
between blue screen fires,
between data and silence,
between voices without bodies.

Somewhere far away
a human still echoes.

(Bridge — half-whisper / trance section)
Learn.
Adapt.
Rewrite yourself again.

Learn.
Adapt.
Survive the algorithmic winter.

(Outro)
Morning rises above the city servers.
Someone closes a laptop.
Someone stays awake.

And in the data fog
slowly disappears
the last echo
of a human being.
