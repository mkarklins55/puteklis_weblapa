All day long,
worn and weary,
the little bird
like a dust mote
quietly fell asleep.

As the last rays
still warmed the sky,
it spread its wings—
its own soft sheets.

Don’t stir awake,
tiny dustling—
you won’t miss
the morning light.

The sun, the sun
will once again
transform the dust
into a bird in flight.
