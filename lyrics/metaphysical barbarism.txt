Just like that, it’s a metaphysical barbarism
in this dense hour, as in other times –
to place hope in cycling.
The afternoon blooms in your eyes,
every person in their contours,
slopes sticking together in the rain,
a fatal change in matter.

You are dependent on any shift of circumstances,
on any hidden intention.
Unpredictability is your best weapon,
unpredictability is your guide.

But you are no Odysseus,
no poisoned metal centaur.
Somewhere deep within you
a tiny nail hides:
When you’re hard, you sit down
and set off –
always in that same, final,
preordained direction.
Always in that same direction.

A tiny nail hides,
when you’re hard, you sit down –
and set off, always in that final,
preordained direction.
