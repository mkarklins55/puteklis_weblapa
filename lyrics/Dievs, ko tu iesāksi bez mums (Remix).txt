God, what will you do without us
in your so-called paradise?
Bored? But it was you
who cast us out.

And you know, it's not so bad —
after that trimmed-back Eden,
it feels good to plunge one’s face
into an icy forest spring;

it feels good to hear the cuckoo call,
let her babble all she likes,
and when she’s babbled long enough,
she’ll get confused and fall silent;

it feels good to hear the wise,
to listen to fools as fools;
it feels good to strike back
and not turn the other ear anymore.

Bees fly like helicopters,
the sun blooms like a yellow dandelion.
...As if you'd just walked out
of the family garden,
having spent your Sunday there.
