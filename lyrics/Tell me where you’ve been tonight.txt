[Verse 1]
Tell me where you’ve been tonight,
City lights don’t tell the truth.
Snow is melting on my coat,
I learned the cold from you.

You said wait, I said fine,
Watched the hours burn away.
Every promise left behind
On a street with no names.

[Chorus]
Last winter, I gave you my heart,
You dropped it somewhere on the way.
This time I’m saving myself,
I won’t bleed for what won’t stay.

Last winter, I gave you my heart,
I watched it freeze and break.
This time I walk it alone,
Someone else can take the blame.

[Verse 2]
Crowded rooms, tired eyes,
Everyone pretending warm.
You lean close, I step back,
I’ve learned what silence costs.

You talk soft, like you care,
Like you didn’t know my name.
I was just a place to rest
When the night got strange.

[Pre-Chorus]
Say my name —
I don’t turn around.
Some things don’t deserve a sound.

[Chorus]
Last winter, I gave you my heart,
You lost it before the dawn.
This time I’m saving myself,
I’m already gone.

[Bridge]
One night the storm went quiet,
No angels, no signs.
Just a pulse inside my chest
Saying: this is mine.

Your cold words don’t scare me now,
I don’t need your light.
I’ll build a fire from the dark
And walk out of the night.

[Final Chorus / Outro]
Last winter, I gave you my heart,
That was the last mistake.
This time I’m saving myself —
I know what not to take.
