When years ago, in silence they burned
Effigies of straw –
Whispers of curses, but the magic was gone.

The straw was damp, the fire crackled,
And now we see – the same old powers,
Changing hats, tying new ribbons –
But the story’s still the same:
Their kitchen’s theirs, the pantry’s ours.

When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
But the lumpens sing: “All is bad, all the same…”
When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
And others quietly march through the city.

Dreams of a second term,
But the wrong dolls burned in the wrong fire.
Ideas tossed out like tempting bait –
And others stride with quiet pride.

Those who wonder – how can this be?
Look close: everything’s in its place.
Who eats, who waits, who stands in line –
There’s a seat at the same old table.

When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
But the lumpens sing: “All is bad, all the same…”
When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
And others quietly march through the city.

Builders, lawyers, bankers and curtains –
They love the hush of muted voices.
Control stands like a doorman at the restaurant door,
But behind the scenes, whispers grow.

Those who build the light slip away in silence –
There’s no room here for those who reveal.
There’s room here for those who hide and mask –
It’s a game where justice doesn’t live.

When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
But the lumpens sing: “All is bad, all the same…”
When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
And others quietly march through the city.

Look around – it’s not just them –
It’s the whole quiet-lipped elite that blooms in hush.
For silence rules when we say nothing,
And then – they speak.

When you burn the doll but forget the soul,
You’re only burning straw – not what truly matters.
When silence reigns among the lines of voters –
Then come harsher winds, a harder hand!
Perhaps it’s time to be more than the lumpens?
