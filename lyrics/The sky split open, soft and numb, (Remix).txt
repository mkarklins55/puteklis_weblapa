🎭 “Under the White Acacias” (Rhythmic Version)
(for cabaret, stomp, punk-folk theatre)

[Verse 1 – march-like]
The sky’s torn open — split again,
We patch it up with rust and pain.
Come gather close, you who have paid,
Beneath these trees where ghosts parade.

[Chorus – stomp rhythm]
  You are alive!
  Yes — you are alive!
  One horse still runs,
  One stag still rides!

[Verse 2 – aggressive, biting]
To swallow fire? That’s no disgrace,
Just pour some beer and keep your pace.
We bite the flames in drunken tune,
Then chase dead moths beneath the moon.

[Verse 3 – accusatory]
Hey buttoned boys — hide all your steel,
Before this crowd forgets to kneel.
Our flag is stitched from skirts once worn,
It flaps like sin, like rage reborn!

[Bridge – sharp, rhythmic bounce]
You got your guts? You got your meat?
Or frogs that croak inside the heat?
We’re all the same, from priest to whore —
You think it’s different past that door?

[Chorus – repeated, faster]
  You are alive!
  Yes — you are alive!
  One horse still runs,
  One spot survives!

[Verse 4 – manic, carnival tone]
Your stare is glass, your grip is fake,
You’ll snap before this system breaks.
But we — we fly with burning lace,
A flag of thighs and powdered face.

[Outro – returns to original pace, grim smile]
Again, again — the sky is torn,
Again we mend with flags forlorn.
Come closer now, you who have paid,
Beneath the blooms that never fade.

Atdzejots Māris Čaklais "DZIESMA PAR KARUSELI"
