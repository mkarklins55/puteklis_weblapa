🎵 “The Soup Can Fuck Itself”
[Verse 1]
Time cracks like cheap porcelain —
no bang, just the indecency of silence.
Someone stirs the air with a fucking plan
and calls it “teamwork”.
The spoon spins alone,
a prophet without followers.

[Verse 2]
Maps are written in ego grease.
Every path is a compromise tattooed in dust.
You dare improvise? That’s a sin here.
The air gets tight when the plan goes off-script.
God’s not dead, just micromanaging the cutlery drawer.

[Pre-Chorus]
They say it’s a shared ride —
but every seat comes with a leash.
You blink, you’re a threat.
You breathe, you’re rewriting the fucking narrative.

[Chorus]
The soup can fuck itself.
Too many hands in the broth,
too many gods whispering, “My way.”
Control is a virtue now.
Freedom? That’s for people who don’t fear being wrong.

[Verse 3]
Touch is tyranny.
Silence is loaded.
You say nothing — still, you disrupt the ritual.
Some people love harmony
as long as they wrote the damn melody.

[Bridge – Spoken]
Structure’s a coffin with velvet lining.
Call it “safety,” call it “order.”
I call it fear in a pretty fucking hat.
You want to build something together?
Start by shutting the fuck up
and letting it break first.

[Chorus Repeat]
The soup can fuck itself.
Your “collaboration” is just dictatorship in yoga pants.
Every spoon a manifesto.
Every absence a mutiny.
Let it boil over.
Let it stain the fucking tablecloth of your certainty.

[Outro – Whispered, smirking]
Nothing’s ruined.
It just refused to become your idea of edible.
