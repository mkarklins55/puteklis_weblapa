With strange, crooked, broken movements,
With strange clothes, from who-knows-what woven,
With strange eyes, like a hawthorn forest’s gleam,
Past our house walked a stranger unseen.

And you said to me: that man is pretending,
Why does he speak through the late evening’s ending?
He stopped at the gate, called out the dog’s name,
And tossed it a will-o’-the-wisp flame.
