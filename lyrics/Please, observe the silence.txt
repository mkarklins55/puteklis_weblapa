Please, observe the silence
No, please, truly the silence
Stop slamming doors and raising voices in sudden hours
Don’t stamp your feet – who still pounds the table with a fist?
Please, keep the quiet
Here lies my childhood’s ghost
And if you listen closely,
You’ll hear eleven more just like it – trembling at each sound

Of course, we cannot ask the world
To watch television in a hush
For those with roaring, thundering laughter to stop laughing
For neighbors not to quarrel and drill
For dogs not to bark and passengers not to eat on the tram

But if we and our traumas
Ever turn into a political party
Or a military force –
I promise, at least in this state,
We will pass the law of silence
Or real, long hours of quiet
And everyone will be called
To lay down weapons
Forks, knives – even spoons

 
Drivers will stop,
But through closed windows, they may still hear the radio
Saws, hammers, all those tools
Will rest in their designated places
If you drop something, it will be a violation –
But you’ll only receive a warning

No one will be allowed to clap
Forgive me – not even to sing
Only children will live as they always have
Until they learn how important it is to keep still

For each crime against the silence
You will be sentenced
To half an hour of awkward conversation
With an old classmate from elementary school

Atdzejots no Anna Belkovska
